### 2.2 同步 JQL（✅ 已确认照搬看板，Q-D1；已写入 03 第 6 章 `qd.jira.board-jql`）

```
category = 10200 AND statusCategory != Done AND (
  (project IN (VERA2202M, VERA2203M, VERA2307MW, VERA2307M, VERA2409M, VERA2505M, VERA2506M,
               VERA2507M, VERA2614M, VERA2625M, VERA2626M, VERA2632M, VERA2633M)
   AND issuetype IN (10001, 10101))
  OR
  (project IN (BUGA2202M, BUGA2203M, BUGA2307MW, BUGA2307M, BUGA2409M, BUGA2505M, BUGA2506M,
               BUGA2507M, BUGA2614M, BUGA2625M, BUGA2626M, BUGA2632M, BUGA2633M)
   AND issuetype = 1)
) AND (assignee IN membersOf("magic-admin") OR assignee IN membersOf("magic-dev")
       OR assignee IN membersOf("magic-tpm") OR assignee IN membersOf("magic_devops"))
```

> 用类型 ID 代替类型名称，避免名称修改导致匹配失效；看板查询日后若调整，需同步更新配置项 `qd.jira.board-jql`。
